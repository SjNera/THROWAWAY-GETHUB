Online Assessment and Payment

How to register?

  1. Visit https://payment.marikina.gov.ph
  2. Click Sign Up to create an account
  3. Input Account Information, Please note that the password must contain an uppercase, lowercase, number and special character.
  4. An email will be sent to verify the registration.

How to apply and pay for New Business?

  1. Visit https://payment.marikina.gov.ph
  2. Sign in using your registered email and password
  3. Select Business Permit & Licensing Office
  4. Click Register NEW Business
  5. Data Privacy Consent click I Agree
  6. Fill out Business and Taxpayer Information
  7. Add Line of Business
  8. Type Line of Business then click Search
  9. Select Line of Business then click Next
  10. Input Capitalization then click Save
  11. Fill out Rented/Lease information only if Business Place is rented
  12. Upload Requirements
  13. Click the checkbox if agree then click Submit New Application
  14. Select Business for Payment then click Compute Business Tax
  15. Click Proceed to Checkout
  16. Select Payment Method
  17. Click the checkbox if you agree then click Pay Now
  18. Terms and Conditions
  19. Click the checkbox if you agree then click Proceed to Payment
  20. An email confirmation will be sent after successful transaction.

How to apply and pay for Renewal of Business?

  1. Visit https://payment.marikina.gov.ph
  2. Sign in using your registered email and password
  3. Select Business Permit & Licensing Office
  4. Input Business Account Number
  5. Click Renew button
  6. Input the Actual / Annual Declared Gross Sales and attached your Financial Statement and Quarterly Vat then Click Submit
  7. Select business and quarter that you want to pay then click Compute Business Tax
  8. Click Proceed to Checkout
  9. Select Payment Method,
  10. Click the checkbox if you agree then click Pay Now
  11. Terms and Conditions
  12. Click the checkbox if you agree then click Proceed to Payment
  13. An email confirmation will be sent after successful transaction.

Where are the Payment options?

  1. You can use your Credit card, Debit Card, E-wallet (Gcash and Paymaya) and Bayad App.
