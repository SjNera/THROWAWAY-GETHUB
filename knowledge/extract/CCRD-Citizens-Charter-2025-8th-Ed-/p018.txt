9. Registration of Regular and Timely Certificate of
   Death Via QC Death Registration Online (DRO)
 This is the process of registering the Certificates of Death of deceased persons whose death
 occurred in Quezon City within thirty (30) days from the date of death through the QC Death
 Registration Online portal at the QC E-services.
