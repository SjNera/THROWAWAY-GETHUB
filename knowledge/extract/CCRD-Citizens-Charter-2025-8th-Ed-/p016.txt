7. Delayed Registration of Certificate of Marriage
    The process of registering the Certificates of Marriage constituents who married in Quezon
    City and which were not filed within 15 days following the solemnization of marriage or 30
    days, for those exempted from marriage license requirement.
   Office or Division:              City Civil Registry Department - Marriage Registration Division (Counter 12)
   Classification:                  Simple
   Type of Transaction:             G2C – Government to Client
   Who may avail:                   QC constituents, Non-QC Residents (who were married in Quezon City)
