AMENDMENT OF BUSINESS PERMIT
Not all business information stays accurate for its entire existence. Some change addresses, while some
change their business activity. In which case, clients are responsible to apply corresponding amendments to
their Business Permits accordingly pursuant to Article 21, Section 77B of the Quezon City Revenue Code, as
amended by City Ordinance No. SP-3013, S-2021.
