2. GENDER  MAINSTREAMING:  PROGRAM/PROJECT/ACTIVITY AND  POLICY-
  MAKING

   The Gender mainstreaming is a strategy to realize gender equality. It involves the integration of a gender
    perspective  into  the  preparation,  design,  implementation,  monitoring and  evaluation  of  policies,
    regulatory measures and spending programs, with a view to promoting equality between women and
   men, and combating discrimination.
