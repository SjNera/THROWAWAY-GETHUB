2. VAXCERTPH APPLICATION ASSISTANCE


   VaxCertPH is the official digital vaccination certificate for Filipinos and non-Filipinos vaccinated in the
    Philippines that may be used for international and domestic travel. This system is developed by the
   Department of Information and Communications Technology (DICT).

   Only those vaccinated at Marikina LGU vaccination sites can be rectified by Marikina LGU. For those
    vaccinated outside Marikina, they will have to coordinate with the concerned LGU or country where they
   were vaccinated.
