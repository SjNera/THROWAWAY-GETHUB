2.  CRISIS INTERVENTION SERVICES: WOMEN AND CHILDREN'S SHELTER


     a.  Protective Custody and Therapeutic Care Services


The Women and Children’s Shelter was established to provide immediate temporary custody and
therapeutic care services to survivors of violence, specifically women and girls, with safety and security
issues.
