1. CAPACITY BUILDING/TRAINING

   The Capacity Building/Training is a series of activities given to all government employees and citizens of
    Marikina to improve their awareness and knowledge on gender-related laws and principles, especially
    those who are part of the mechanisms and systems that formulate and implement programs that
   promotes gender mainstreaming, gender equality and women empowerment, and to improve their skills
     in providing crisis intervention and care services to clients.
