                                                Marikina Citizen’s Charter




        MARIKINA CITY LIBRARY


                      CITIZEN’S CHARTER
                        2022




MANDATE      :      Provide an easy and updated access to information.



VISION            :     To become a premiere public library imbued with sound values and a strong
                 commitment to lifelong learning and information literacy.



MISSION         :     To promote the moral and intellectual well-being and elevate the literacy
                       level of every Marikenos, to preserve and make available the vast resources
                     of Marikina City Library to the public.
