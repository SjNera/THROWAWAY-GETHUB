22. R.A. 9048 - Petition for Correction of Clerical Error (CCE)
    This is the process of allowing the document owner to correct clerical or typographical
    errors in his/her Civil Registration Documents.
    (Birth Certificate, Marriage Certificate, Death Certificate)

  Classification:             Complex
  Type of Transaction:        G2C - Government to Client
  Who may avail:              Parents/document owners/ Attorneys-in-Fact who have discrepancies in the
