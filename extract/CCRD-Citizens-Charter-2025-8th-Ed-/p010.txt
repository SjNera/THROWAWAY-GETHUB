4. Delayed Registration of Certificate of Live
   Birth Via QC Birth Registration Online (BRO)
    This is the process of registering the Certificates of Live Birth of Filipino citizens, born in
    Quezon City, after the thirty (30)-day filing period from the date of birth of the person who
    have no existing record in the Register of Births of the City, through the QC Birth Registration
    Online portal at the QC E-services.
 Office or Division:                City Civil Registry Department - Birth Registration Division (Counter 18)
 Classification:                    Simple
 Type of Transaction:               G2C - Government to Client
 Who may avail:                     All Government/Private Hospitals, Maternity & Lying-In Clinics/other birth
                                    attendants, QC Constituents
