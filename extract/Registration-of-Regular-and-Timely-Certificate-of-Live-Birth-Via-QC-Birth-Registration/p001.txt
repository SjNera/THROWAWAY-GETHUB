2. Registration of Regular and Timely Certificate of Live Birth
   Via QC Birth Registration Online (BRO)
   This is the process of registering Certificates of Live Birth of Filipino citizens, born in Quezon City,
   within thirty (30) days from the date of birth through the QC Birth Registration Online portal at the
   QC E-services.
