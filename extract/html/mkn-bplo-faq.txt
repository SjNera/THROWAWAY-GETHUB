Online Assessment and Payment
How to register?
Visit https://payment.marikina.gov.ph
Click Sign Up to create an account
Input Account Information, Please note that the password must
 contain an uppercase, lowercase, number and special character.
An email will be sent to verify the registration.
How to apply and pay for New Business?
 Visit 
https://payment.marikina.gov.ph
 Sign in using your registered email and password 
 Select Business Permit & Licensing Office
 Click Register NEW Business
 Data Privacy Consent click I Agree
 Fill out Business and Taxpayer Information
 Add Line of Business
Type Line of Business then click Search
Select Line of Business then click Next 
Input Capitalization then click Save
Fill out Rented/Lease information only if Business Place is
 rented
Upload Requirements
Click the checkbox if agree then click Submit New Application
Select Business for Payment then click Compute Business Tax
Click Proceed to Checkout
Select Payment Method
Click the checkbox if you agree then click Pay Now 
Terms and Conditions
Click the checkbox if you agree then click Proceed to Payment
An email confirmation will be sent after successful
 transaction.
How to apply and pay for Renewal of Business?
Visit 
https://payment.marikina.gov.ph
Sign in using your registered email and password 
Select Business Permit & Licensing Office
Input Business Account Number
Click Renew button
Input the Actual / Annual Declared Gross Sales and attached
 your Financial Statement and Quarterly Vat then Click Submit
Select business and quarter that you want to pay then click
 Compute Business Tax
Click Proceed to Checkout
Select Payment Method, 
Click the checkbox if you agree then click Pay Now
Terms and Conditions
Click the checkbox if you agree then click Proceed to
 Payment
An email confirmation will be sent after successful
 transaction.
Where are the Payment options?
You can use your Credit card, Debit Card, E-wallet (Gcash
 and
 Paymaya) and Bayad App.
