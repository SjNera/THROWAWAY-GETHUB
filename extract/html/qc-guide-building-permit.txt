Log in to QC E-Services to generate the following:
Building Permit Application forms
Checklist of Requirements
Click ‘Building Permit One-Stop Shop’.
Electronically submit the documents for pre-evaluation.
Schedule an appointment to physically submit the documentary requirements based on the checklist.
Physically submit or drop-off the documentary requirements on the date of your appointment via the kiosk machine located at the Ground Floor of the Department of the Building Official, Civic Center D, Quezon City Hall Compound.
Present a valid ID to claim the official assessment and fees or the ‘Order of Payment’.
Pay the required fee at the Counter 12 of the City Treasurer’s Office, located at the Ground Floor of the Department of the Building Official.
