Filing a renewal application is required one (1) year from the issuance date of your permit.
REQUIREMENTS:
Official Receipt (machine validated) issued on the current year, from the City Treasurer’s Office
Barangay Business Clearance (if already secured)
Previous Business Permit
Authorization Form (for application that will be processed by an authorized representative
ONLINE APPLICATION:
Pay your business tax.
via the QC E-Services portal
via the QC E-Services Kiosks located at the Quezon CIty Hall Compound
Select ‘Renewal’.
Input Mayor’s Permit Number and Business Tax Official Receipt of the current year.
Wait for notification via email to claim the renewed permit in-person, or have it delivered via a courier of choice.
Note: The online system is capable of auto-renewing a business permit if the establishment is compliant with all local regulatory requirements at the time of renewal.
