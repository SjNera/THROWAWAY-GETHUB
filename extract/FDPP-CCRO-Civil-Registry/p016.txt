5.  REPUBLIC ACT 9048  - Processing of Petitions For Correction of Clerical /Typographical Error and
   Change of First Name in Birth Certificate
    Republic Act No. 9048 authorizes the Municipal Civil Registrar or the Consul General to correct a clerical
    or typographical error in an entry and/ or change of first name, in the civil registry without a need of a
     judicial order. An administrative remedy in nature,  it is a departure from the usual judicial process in
    correcting clerical errors of changing an entry in civil registry documents.  It is aimed at according
    petitioners an expeditious and cheaper way of correcting errors found in her/ his record.
