   4. COLLECTION OF TRANSFER TAX
      Provides over-the-counter payment for transfer tax payors.
      Marikina City imposes a tax rate of seventy five percent (75%) of one percent (1%) on the sale,
       donation, barter, or any mode of transferring ownership of the real property within territorial
        jurisdiction of Marikina based on the total consideration involved in the sale of the property or of the
         fair market value in case the monetary consideration involved in the transfer is not substantial,
      whichever is higher. LGC (Section 135) & Revenue Code of Marikina Ord. 085 S-2015 (Sec. 21 -
       24).
