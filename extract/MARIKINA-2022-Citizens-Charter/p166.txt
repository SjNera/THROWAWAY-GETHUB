                                             Marikina Citizen’s Charter





      OFFICE OF THE VICE MAYOR


                   CITIZEN’S CHARTER
                       2022





MANDATE       :    Implement Article Two Section 445 (a) of Republic Act No. 7160,
                   otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991



VISION            :    To promote harmonious relation with the executive branch; provide
                    leadership and direction to the Sangguniang Panlungsod;  formulate
                 and enact legislative measures to create vital, healthy lives for the
                      families and communities now and for the future.



MISSION         :    To provide assistance to Chief Executive and support its executive
                   agenda, as well as attaining an  efficient and effective measures
                  through local legislation.
