                                            Marikina Citizen’s Charter





 MARIKINA CITY TRADE, INDUSTRY AND
   INVESTMENT PROMOTIONS OFFICE


                    CITIZEN’S CHARTER
                       2022



MANDATE      :    To function as the City's Investment promotion to formulate plans and
                 programs that will concentrate on the strategies and development of
                   business in the city.



VISION           :    To become a city with vibrant investment and job opportunities for
                     inclusive growth.



MISSION        :    To provide an enabling environment for enterprises to develop and
                     flourish in the city.
