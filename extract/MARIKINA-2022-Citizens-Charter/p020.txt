1.  CRISIS INTERVENTION SERVICES: MARIKINA REFERRAL SYSTEM

    The Crisis Intervention Services provide an organized care services for victim-survivors of gender
     violence through the Marikina Referral System.  It also develops and implements interventions,
     capacity buildings and self-care programs for service providers.
