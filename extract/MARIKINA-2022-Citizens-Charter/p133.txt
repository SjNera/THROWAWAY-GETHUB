1.  ISSUANCE OF ZONING CERTIFICATE FOR BUSINESS


    Issuance of Zoning Certificate is a prerequisite document before a person can get a business permit to
    ensure that the business is in conformity with the existing Comprehensive Land Use Plan, Zoning
    Ordinance and other pertaining laws and ordinances.

 Office or Division:           City Planning and Development Office

  Classification:              Simple

 Type of Transaction:        Government to Business Sector

 Who may avail:             Business owner
