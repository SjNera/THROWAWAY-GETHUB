                CITY ENVIRONMENTAL MANAGEMENT OFFICE

                       EXTERNAL SERVICES

1. Special Garbage Collection Services “Special Trips”


   A special service provided by the city government through the City Environmental Management Office to
   accommodate requests from private individuals or entities for special garbage collection services or special
     trips “extra travel of garbage collection trucks to and from a garbage collection point to the city garbage
    Transfer Station or Centralized Materials Recovery  Facility, outside of  its designated schedule” and
    imposing charges.



  Office or Division:           City Environmental Management Office
  Classification:              Simple
  Type of Transaction:      G2C – Government to Citizen
                      G2B – Government to Business Sector
 Who may avail:              Residents and Business Establishments of Marikina
