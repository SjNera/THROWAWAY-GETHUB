     business owners with regards to their needs on how to open their business , avail trainings, secure loans,
     promotion of products, and other business- related transactions.

  Office or Division:           Marikina City Trade Industry and Investment Promotions Office
  Classification:              Simple
  Type of Transaction:      G2B – Government to Business Sector
