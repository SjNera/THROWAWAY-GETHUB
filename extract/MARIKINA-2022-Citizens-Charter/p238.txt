                                                Marikina Citizen’s Charter3.  REGISTRATION OF DEATH CERTIFICATE
      It shall be the responsibility of the spouse or any nearest relative who has knowledge of the death to report
    the same within forty-eight (48) hours if the deceased died without medical assistance. The Health Officer
    shall examine the deceased and shall certify as to the cause of death and direct the registration of the death
    to the officer of the Civil Registrar within the Reglementary period of thirty (30) days.
