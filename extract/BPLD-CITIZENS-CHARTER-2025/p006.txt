RENEWAL OF BUSINESS PERMIT
Every year, all existing and operating businesses in the city must have their Business Permits renewed.
Pursuant to City Ordinance No. SP-3013, S-2021, “An Ordinance amending the validity of Business Permit from
December 31 of every year to one year from the date of issuance or anniversary date, amending further
Ordinance No. SP-91, S-93, otherwise known as the Quezon City Revenue Code of 1993”, Business Permits shall
be valid one (1) year from the date of issue reflected in the permit.
