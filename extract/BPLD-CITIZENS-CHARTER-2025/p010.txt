ISSUANCE OF SPECIAL PERMIT
Pursuant to Article 21, Section 82 of the QC Revenue Code, as amended, short-term events such as concerts,
Sunday Markets, charitable events, or any other seasonal event of the same nature requires the
organizers/applicants to secure a Special Permit with this Department.
