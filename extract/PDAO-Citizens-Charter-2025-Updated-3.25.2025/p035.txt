       VII. TAX INCENTIVES TO EMPLOYERS WHO HIRE PERSONS WITH
       DISABILITIES (PWDs)
              To promote employment among persons with disabilities within Quezon City, the
              Ordinance No. SP-2940, S-2020 grants tax incentives to employers who hire Persons
              with Disabilities (PWDs) registered in the city. This aims to promote inclusive
              employment while providing financial relief to businesses that support workplace
              diversity.
