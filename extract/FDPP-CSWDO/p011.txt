 4.   SOCIAL WELFARE PROGRAMS

        a.  ADOPTION
          Based from Presidential Decree 603 article 3 on the rights of the child paragraph 2 states that
           “Every child has the right to a wholesome family life that will provide him with love, care and
            understanding, guidance and counseling, and moral and material security”. The dependent or
          abandoned child shall be provided with the nearest substitute for a home. The Social Welfare
          and Development Office of Marikina City provides assistance to those prospective adoptive
           parents who wishes to adopt a child.
